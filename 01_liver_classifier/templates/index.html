      What this model can and cannot show

      Trained on a small regional dataset from India. The positive label means ILPD Selector = 1, not cirrhosis or a specific disease stage. Scores are uncalibrated. Duplicate profiles are removed before splitting; there are no patient IDs to rule out all related records. Demographic coverage and clinical generalization are limited.

      Submitted values are not saved or logged by this application. Models run locally. No claim of clinical validation.
