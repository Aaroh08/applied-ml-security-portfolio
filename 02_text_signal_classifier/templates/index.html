Read the tags carefully

The six tags—atypical, bipolar, major depressive, no, postpartum and psychotic—are inherited from the source dataset. They are not a validated diagnostic taxonomy. A high score can simply reflect a topic word. The “no” tag does not establish wellness or rule out distress.

TF-IDF captures word and phrase patterns. The second model uses frozen DistilBERT embeddings with logistic regression. Both can fail on negation, quotes, sarcasm and new domains. No user IDs or timestamps are provided, so user-separated evaluation cannot be verified.
